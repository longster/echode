    Guides

    Short, practical answers for Deaf life in San Antonio.

    New to San Antonio's Deaf community? Start here

    Three good first doors: the Deaf Night Out San Antonio Facebook group, the main hub for Deaf Coffee Chat, open-captioned movies, and community announcements; Family Deaf Church, a Deaf-led church worshipping in American Sign Language; and Aid the Silent, a nonprofit helping deaf and hard-of-hearing children and teens with hearing aids, therapy, and ASL lessons.

    How to find an ASL interpreter in San Antonio

    For medical, legal, work, or school interpreting, start with a local agency: Deaf Interpreter Services (since 1993, onsite and by video, around the clock), Alamo City Interpreters (founded 2014), or Deaf Link for on-demand video remote interpreting, all in the directory above. Book as early as you can, and ask for BEI certified interpreters when the setting is formal.

    Where to learn ASL in San Antonio

    For structured classes, San Antonio College offers ASL and interpreter training programs, including the Sign Language Interpreter A.A.S. For practice, the Friday ASL Coffee Chat is a signing social. For children, Sunshine Cottage School serves deaf children from birth through fifth grade, with audiology and speech-language therapy.

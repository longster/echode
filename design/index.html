Echode: Establishing Connections

Design

One system for Echode and every city page. The layout geometry mirrors the HDN site, so the whole network feels like one family... the brand (logo, colors, aurora, pill nav) is Echode's own. If a value lives here, pages do not re-derive it.

Logo

Echode is ECHO + CODE... spreading waves through coding. The split O is the signal. The wordmark is custom and stays as drawn.

Primary, for light backgrounds

Reverse, for dark backgrounds

  • Clear space around the logo: at least the height of the O on all sides.
  • Minimum width on screen: 120px. The header uses it at 22px tall.
  • Do not recolor, stretch, outline, or rearrange the letters.
  • On a city page, the logo links to the Echode homepage and the city name beside it links to that city's home.
  • Slogan: Establishing Connections. Signal and connection language, never "voice."

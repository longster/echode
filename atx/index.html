FAQ

Is Echode Austin open?

Not officially. Austin is in the collecting stage: seeded listings are in, and the page opens fully once the community starts adding to it and events recur. Houston is the live example of what an open city looks like.

How do I get listed?

Use the Suggest button. Every suggestion goes to a review queue first, and community listings are always free. Businesses get the same review; paid visibility, when it exists, is always labeled.

Who runs this?

Echode is a one person community project run by Long Duong in Houston, the same person behind HoustonDeafNetwork. Not a nonprofit, no office, just a directory built to last.
